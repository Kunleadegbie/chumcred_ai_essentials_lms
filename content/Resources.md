## 📌 Templates (Quick Links)

### 1) Prompt Template (CITSK+)
**File:** `templates/Prompt_Template_CITSK.md`  
**When to use:**  
- Week 2 prompt engineering tasks  
- Any time AI output is poor or unclear  
- When you need structured results (tables, steps, checklists)

**Why it helps:**  
It forces your prompt to include the 5 layers AI needs to respond accurately:
Context → Intent → Tone → Structure → Keywords  
Plus a recap request to confirm understanding.

---

### 2) Workflow Template (SMART Model)
**File:** `templates/Workflow_Template_SMART.md`  
**When to use:**  
- Week 3 workflow assignments  
- Anytime you want AI to save time daily  
- When building repeatable systems for work or business

**Why it helps:**  
It turns one-off AI usage into a repeatable workflow:
Scan → Model → Automate → Review → Transform
