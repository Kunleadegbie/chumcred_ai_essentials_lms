## 🧠 What is AI? (Simple Definition)
AI is a tool that can:
- generate text, ideas, summaries, plans
- help you draft documents
- support decision-making (not replace your thinking)

**Important:** AI is not always correct. You must verify important facts.

---

## ✅ What AI is Good At
AI is excellent for:
- writing drafts (emails, reports, proposals)
- summarizing long content
- brainstorming ideas
- turning rough notes into clear structure
- creating templates and checklists

---

## ⚠️ What AI is NOT Good At
AI can:
- invent facts (hallucination)
- give outdated information
- sound confident even when wrong

**Rule:** If it matters (money, health, legal, exams), verify.

---

## 🔒 AI Safety: What NOT to Paste into AI
Never paste:
- passwords, PINs, OTP codes
- private bank details
- confidential company data
- private customer information

Use AI responsibly.

---

## ✅ Your Week 1 Skill: “Verify + Improve”
After any AI output:
1) **Check accuracy** (facts, names, numbers)  
2) **Check completeness** (did it answer all parts?)  
3) **Check format** (table/steps/template?)  
4) **Improve prompt** (if output is weak)
